# Code of conduct

Buddy is built for families of children with ADHD, and the people who use it, report bugs or send
changes are often tired parents. Be kind to them.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Buddy to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability (ADHD included), ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being patient and friendly, especially with newcomers and people writing in their second
  language.
- Respecting different opinions and experiences, and giving feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accepting responsibility for mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them.
- Treating any family details people share (about their children, diagnoses or medicines) as
  private.

Examples of behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Mocking anyone's or anyone's child's diagnosis, health or parenting.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as an address, email or details about their
  children, without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and commit
messages, and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at **[kim.ipsen+buddy@outlook.dk](mailto:kim.ipsen+buddy@outlook.dk)**. Don't report it in
a public issue. Every report is reviewed promptly and kept confidential, and the reporter's privacy
is respected.

For security problems, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides what is unacceptable and how to respond, and may remove, edit or reject
comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code. Depending on
how serious and how repeated the behaviour is, the response ranges from:

1. **Correction**: a private, written warning explaining what was wrong. A public apology may be
   requested.
2. **Warning**: a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, such as no interaction with
   the people involved for a period.
3. **Temporary ban**: no interaction with the project for a set time.
4. **Permanent ban**: no further interaction with the project.
